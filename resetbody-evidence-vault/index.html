The verdict system

Simple labels. Nuanced evidence.

Every audit separates the practical verdict from the certainty behind it. A useful answer can still have limitations.

↑Protects

Supports muscle retention

A practice or factor that can help preserve muscle or training performance in the stated context.

↔Neutral

No clear meaningful edge

No reliable universal effect, or the practical impact is small, uncertain, or context-dependent.

↓Costs

May work against retention

A choice that can increase the risk of losing muscle or undermine performance in the stated context.
